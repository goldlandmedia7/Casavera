ABOUT CASAVERA

"We treat every project as though we were building it for our own family — in the materials we choose, the trades we trust, and the deadlines we keep."

Founder, Casavera Interiors

Casavera Interiors is a Dubai-based design and fit-out practice working across residential, retail, office and hospitality spaces. Our in-house architects, designers and site teams carry a project from first sketch through to the final handover — one point of contact, one standard of craft.

WHY CASAVERA

Built for accountability

No handoffs between separate design, build and furnishing firms — just one team responsible for the result.

In-house execution

Design, fit-out and furniture teams sit under one roof, so decisions made in a design meeting reach the site the same week.

Material sourcing

Commercial & residential range

From a single boutique fit-out to a full villa renovation, the same process and site discipline apply.

Transparent estimates
